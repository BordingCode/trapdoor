# Trapdoor

**[Play it →](https://bordingcode.github.io/trapdoor/)**

A short troll platformer. Every level looks like a harmless tutorial: a flat floor, a
door, three seconds of walking. Then the floor gives way, or the spikes come up, or the
door walks off.

24 hand-made levels across three chapters, plus three you have to earn. Instant respawn,
infinite retries, a death counter that will judge you. Works offline, installs to a phone
home screen.

**Nerve.** Every level hides one, always somewhere the safe route doesn't go — floating
over the trapdoor, at dart height, inside the corridor the crusher is about to fill.
Taking it is a choice to be in danger on purpose. Spend one for a *glimpse*: a second and
a half in which the level shows you everything it was about to do. Collect a whole
chapter's worth and it opens a level that shouldn't exist.

One of those levels lies to your glimpse.

**Controls** — arrows / WASD to move, space to jump, E to glimpse, R to restart, Esc to
pause. On a phone, the thumb pads and the ◆ button.

Built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no framework, no build step, no dependencies.
