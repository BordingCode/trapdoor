# Trapdoor

**[Play it →](https://bordingcode.github.io/trapdoor/)**

A short troll platformer. Every level looks like a harmless tutorial: a flat floor, a
door, three seconds of walking. Then the floor gives way, or the spikes come up, or the
door walks off.

24 hand-made levels across three chapters. Instant respawn, infinite retries, a death
counter that will judge you. Works offline, installs to a phone home screen.

**Controls** — arrows / WASD to move, space to jump, R to restart, Esc to pause. On a
phone, the three thumb pads.

Built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no framework, no build step, no dependencies.
